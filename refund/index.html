Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mentally sells a digital product (the Mentally script, 8 €) delivered instantly after payment. Because of that, our refund policy is strictly defined as follows.

1. Digital goods

  • Once your licence key has been generated and delivered, the sale is final.
  • No refund is issued for a change of mind, for not reading the FAQ, or for an executor that is not supported.

2. Cryptocurrency payments

  • Cryptocurrency transactions are non-refundable because blockchain transfers are irreversible. Please double-check every detail before sending funds.

3. When we do refund or replace

At our discretion, a refund or a replacement key may be granted when:

  • Your key was not delivered because of a technical error on our side, and we cannot deliver it within a reasonable time.
  • You were charged twice for a single order. The duplicate charge is refunded to the original payment method (crypto network fees excluded).
  • The script does not work at all on a supported setup and our support cannot fix it.

4. How to ask

  • Contact support first, with your order e-mail and a short description of the problem, within 7 days of your purchase.
  • We will try to fix the issue before anything else. Most problems are solved with a replacement key or a setup fix.

5. Disputes and chargebacks

  • Opening a dispute or chargeback without contacting support first results in an immediate and permanent revocation of your key and a ban from our services.
  • If something is wrong with your order, write to us: it is faster for you and for us.
